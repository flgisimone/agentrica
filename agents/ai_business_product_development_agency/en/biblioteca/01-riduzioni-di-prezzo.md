# Price reduction announcements (art. 17-bis of the Italian Consumer Code)

Source: normattiva · Version: Italian Consumer Code, art. 17-bis, text in force since 2 April 2023 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AIBPD-04, AIBPD-07, AIBPD-08, AIBPD-09

## Notion
Art. 17-bis of the Italian Consumer Code (Codice del consumo), introduced by Legislative Decree no. 26 of 7 March 2023, governs price reduction announcements: the prior price to show is the lowest price the trader applied to consumers in general in the thirty days before the reduction. The rule does not apply to perishable agricultural and food products. In a campaign of progressive discounts without interruption, the reference remains the price before the first reduction.

## How it applies
- In a promotion or pricing proposal with discounts shown to consumers, state that the struck-through price must be the lowest of the previous thirty days.
- For progressive discounts in the same campaign, the reference is the price before the first discount.
- Special cases (new products, launch prices) must be checked in the text of the article and with human legal review.
