# Withdrawal function in online contracts (art. 54-bis of the Italian Consumer Code)

Source: normattiva · Version: Italian Consumer Code, art. 54-bis, introduced by Legislative Decree no. 209 of 31 December 2025 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AIBPD-04, AIBPD-05, AIBPD-07, AIBPD-11

## Notion
Art. 54-bis of the Italian Consumer Code, «Exercising the right of withdrawal from distance contracts concluded through an online interface», introduced by Legislative Decree no. 209 of 31 December 2025, requires the trader to allow withdrawal also through a withdrawal function in the online interface, labelled «withdraw from the contract here» (in Italian «recedere dal contratto qui») or with an equivalent wording. The rules apply from 19 June 2026, to contracts concluded after that date.

## How it applies
- A product that sells online to consumers (subscriptions included) must have the withdrawal function: put it among the requirements and in the acceptance criteria.
- In retention analyses do not propose flows that make withdrawal harder than subscribing or that hide the function.
- Details such as confirmations and timing must be read in the text of the article and confirmed with human legal review.
