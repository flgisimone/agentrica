# Directive (EU) 2024/825: consumers and the green transition

Source: commissione-ue · Version: Directive (EU) 2024/825 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AIBPD-03, AIBPD-04, AIBPD-08

## Notion
Directive (EU) 2024/825 gives consumers better information at the time of purchase on durability, reparability and the legal guarantee, and strengthens the rules against greenwashing and early obsolescence. It was adopted on 28 February 2024; Member States had to transpose it by 27 March 2026 and it applies from 27 September 2026.

## How it applies
- Environmental or sustainability messages about a product (claims, labels, launch communication) must be treated as regulated requirements, not just marketing choices.
- The prohibited practices in detail and the Italian transposition text must be checked on the sources (EUR-Lex and Normattiva) before approving a message.
