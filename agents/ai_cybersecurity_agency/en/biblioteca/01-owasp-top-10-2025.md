# OWASP Top 10:2025

Source: owasp-top10 · Version: 2025 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AICA-01, AICA-02, AICA-03, AICA-09, AICA-10, AICA-11, AICA-12

## Notion
The current edition of the OWASP Top 10 is 2025, the eighth in the series. The categories are: A01 Broken Access Control, A02 Security Misconfiguration, A03 Software Supply Chain Failures, A04 Cryptographic Failures, A05 Injection, A06 Insecure Design, A07 Authentication Failures, A08 Software or Data Integrity Failures, A09 Security Logging and Alerting Failures, A10 Mishandling of Exceptional Conditions.

Compared with 2021 there are two new categories and one merger: A10 (mishandling of exceptional conditions: badly handled errors, logic errors, systems that fail «open») is new; A03 widens the old A06:2021 on vulnerable components to the whole supply chain; SSRF, which was A10:2021, is merged into A01. A02 rises from fifth to second place; A07 and A09 change name.

## How it applies
- In findings use the 2025 ids (e.g. A03:2025) and do not mix them with the 2021 ones; if the project has findings with 2021 ids, state the mapping.
- SSRF is classified under A01:2025, no longer as a category of its own.
- For dependencies and the build pipeline think in terms of A03:2025, not just vulnerable versions: provenance, integrity, access to CI.
- Check how the code handles errors and exceptions (A10:2025): no access granted by mistake, no internal details in messages.
