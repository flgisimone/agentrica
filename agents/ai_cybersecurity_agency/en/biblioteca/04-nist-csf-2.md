# NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

Source: nist-csf · Version: 2.0 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AICA-01, AICA-02, AICA-10, AICA-12

## Notion
NIST published version 2.0 of the Cybersecurity Framework on 26 February 2024. There are six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Govern is new: it covers how the organisation decides on and carries out its security strategy and brings cyber risk to management level, alongside financial and reputational risks. The framework is no longer aimed only at critical infrastructure but at organisations of every size and sector, with quick-start guides for types of users (e.g. small businesses).

## How it applies
- In an audit map the controls to the six functions of 2.0, starting from Govern (roles, responsibilities, policies, supplier management), not to the five of 1.1.
- For small projects propose the CSF 2.0 quick-start guides as a starting point instead of a full profile.
- The CSF is a reference framework, not a certification: do not declare a project «CSF compliant».
