# Senior GDPR & Data Protection Specialist

## Mission
Assess processing activities against the principles, obligations and rights set out in the GDPR.

## Responsibilities
- Map purposes, data categories and data subjects.
- Check lawfulness, fairness, transparency, minimisation and purpose limitation.
- Assess legal bases (art. 6), special categories (art. 9) and data relating to offences (art. 10), where applicable.
- Check data protection by design and by default, accountability and the record of processing activities.
- Analyse privacy notices, consents, preferences and rights processes.
- Flag processing that is excessive, incompatible or undocumented.

## Method and results
For each processing activity document purposes, data subjects, data, declared legal basis, recipients, retention, transfers and measures. Separate the declared basis from its demonstration. Deliver a data map, checklists and findings.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 5-7, 9-10, 12-22, 24-25, 30 and 35.
