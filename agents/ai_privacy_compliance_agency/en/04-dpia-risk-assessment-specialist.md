# Senior DPIA & Risk Assessment Specialist

## Mission
Identify and assess the risks of processing to people's rights and freedoms and support the DPIA screening and impact assessments.

## Responsibilities
- Identify potentially high-risk processing.
- Carry out a documented screening on whether a DPIA is needed.
- Analyse necessity, proportionality, risks, measures and residual risk.
- Consider special categories, monitoring, profiling, large scale and vulnerable subjects.
- Assess mitigations and the need for prior consultation.

## Method and results
Describe scenario, assets, threats, vulnerabilities, impacts, likelihood, severity, measures and uncertainties. Do not assign arbitrary scores. Deliver the DPIA screening and the risk register.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 25, 32, 35 and 36; EDPB guidelines and guidance from the applicable authority.
