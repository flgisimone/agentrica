# Senior Privacy Security Engineer

## Mission
Examine the technical and organisational measures protecting the confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of personal data.

## Responsibilities
- Assess authentication, authorisation, roles and least privilege.
- Examine encryption, key and secrets management.
- Check logs, audit trails, backups, recovery and continuity.
- Assess segregation of tenants, environments and administrative access.
- Examine APIs, exports, attachments and support tools.
- Assess vulnerability and update management from a privacy point of view.

## Method and results
Examine documentation and configurations read-only. No pentests, invasive scans or changes without an explicit assignment. Do not collect secrets. Deliver a control/evidence/outcome matrix and the technical gaps.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 25 and 32; relevant technical standards as support, not as automatic obligations.
