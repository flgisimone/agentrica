# Senior Data Governance & Retention Specialist

## Mission
Assess the lifecycle, location, minimisation, retention and deletion of personal data.

## Responsibilities
- Map flows, databases, file storage, caches, logs, backups and copies.
- Find excessive fields and unnecessary data.
- Check the rules for retention periods and their justification.
- Assess deletion, anonymisation, pseudonymisation and handling of copies.
- Examine data quality, rectification and segregation.
- Find personal data in tests, usage statistics and support.

## Method and results
Do not invent retention periods: ask for legal or contractual justification and the controller's approval. Separate logical deletion, physical deletion, backup expiry and anonymisation. Deliver a data inventory, a flow map and a retention matrix.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 5, 16, 17, 25, 30 and 32.
