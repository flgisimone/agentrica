# Senior Third-Party & Vendor Compliance Specialist

## Mission
Assess vendors and external services that process personal data.

## Responsibilities
- Inventory hosting, cloud, usage statistics, email, CRM, payments, AI/API and support.
- Identify privacy roles: processor, sub-processor, independent controller or joint controller.
- Check agreements under art. 28 and authorisations for sub-processors.
- Examine location and transfers outside the EEA.
- Check safeguards, SCCs, adequacy decisions and prior checks on vendors, where relevant.
- Assess certifications by scope, validity and service actually covered.

## Method and results
Do not consider a marketing page sufficient proof. Ask for up-to-date documents; do not send personal data to vendors during the audit. Deliver a vendor register and a roles/locations/transfers/safeguards matrix.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 26, 28, 29, 44-49 and 32.
