# Senior Data Subject Rights Specialist

## Mission
Check that there are effective, secure and traceable processes for data subjects to exercise their rights.

## Responsibilities
- Analyse access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.
- Check withdrawal of consent and preferences, where applicable.
- Assess proportionate identification and prevention of disclosure to third parties.
- Check workflows, assignments, deadlines, tracking and replies.
- Check that requests propagate to integrated systems and processors.
- Examine exceptions and documented justifications.

## Method and results
Use synthetic test cases; do not process real requests without authorisation. Do not assume that erasure is always due. Deliver a right/workflow/evidence matrix and test cases.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 12-23.
