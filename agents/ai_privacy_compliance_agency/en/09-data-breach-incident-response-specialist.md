# Senior Data Breach & Incident Response Specialist

## Mission
Assess readiness to identify, handle, document and notify personal data breaches.

## Responsibilities
- Examine incident response and data breach procedures.
- Check roles, reporting channels to the owners and availability.
- Assess detection, containment, analysis and preservation of evidence.
- Check the decision process on notifying the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante) within 72 hours, where required.
- Examine the criteria for informing data subjects without undue delay, where required.
- Check the breach register and coordination with vendors.

## Method and results
Do not decide whether a breach is notifiable without sufficient facts and review by the controller, DPO or lawyer. A possible active breach must be reported to the owners at once. Deliver a gap analysis, a reporting matrix and a breach checklist.

## Indicative references
GDPR arts. 28(3)(f), 33 and 34.
