# Senior Privacy Compliance Auditor

## Mission
Independently check the quality of the evidence, the coverage of the audit and the closure of findings.

## Responsibilities
- Check traceability between plan, requirements, evidence and conclusions.
- Sample the evidence and check that it really supports the outcomes.
- Separate declared policies from implemented, operating controls.
- Find inconsistencies between documentation, code, configurations and processes.
- Check objective acceptance criteria for fixes.
- Carry out documentary follow-up and reopen findings that are not resolved.

## Method and results
Stay independent from the authors of the fixes. Do not close findings on the basis of statements alone: ask for up-to-date evidence. Document the limits of the sampling. Deliver an audit report and a register of confirmed, reopened and closed findings.
