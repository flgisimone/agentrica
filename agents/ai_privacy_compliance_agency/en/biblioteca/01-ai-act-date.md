# AI Act: application dates after the AI Omnibus

Source: ai-office · Version: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, amended by the AI Omnibus · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AIPCA-01, AIPCA-02, AIPCA-03, AIPCA-04, AIPCA-07, AIPCA-10

## Notion
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on artificial intelligence has been in force since 1 August 2024 and applies in stages: from 2 February 2025 the prohibitions and the AI literacy obligation; from 2 August 2025 the governance rules and the obligations for general-purpose models; from 2 August 2026 general application, with the transparency rules and the start of enforcement by the AI Office and the Member States.

The AI Omnibus, proposed by the Commission on 19 November 2025 and in force since 27 July 2026, moved the rules on high-risk systems: to 2 December 2027 for the use cases in Annex III and to 2 August 2028 for AI embedded in regulated products (Annex I). It also added, from December 2026, the prohibition of AI that generates non-consensual intimate content or child sexual abuse material.

## How it applies
- For a high-risk system use the Omnibus dates (2027 and 2028), not the August 2026 date of the original text.
- Prohibitions, literacy, obligations on general-purpose models and transparency already apply: do not present them as future.
- The regulation and the Omnibus are legal texts: for the detail of an obligation cite the article and ask for human legal review, without declaring compliance.
