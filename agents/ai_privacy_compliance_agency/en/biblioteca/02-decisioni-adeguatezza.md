# Commission adequacy decisions

Source: commissione-dati · Version: list as of 2026-11-19 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AIPCA-01, AIPCA-02, AIPCA-03, AIPCA-07, AIPCA-10

## Notion
The countries and organisations with a Commission adequacy decision are: Andorra, Argentina, Brazil, Canada (commercial organisations), Faroe Islands, Guernsey, Israel, Isle of Man, Japan, Jersey, New Zealand, Republic of Korea, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States (only the organisations certified under the Data Privacy Framework), Uruguay and the European Patent Organisation.

Recent decisions: European Patent Organisation (15 July 2025); United Kingdom, renewal of the 2021 decisions (19 December 2025); Brazil, mutual adequacy (27 January 2026); Korea, first review completed with a positive outcome (23 July 2026). The EU-US Data Privacy Framework dates from 10 July 2023. With Kenya a mutual adequacy arrangement is in progress, not yet adopted.

## How it applies
- For a transfer to the United States check that the vendor is certified under the Data Privacy Framework; if it is not, other safeguards are needed (e.g. standard contractual clauses).
- Brazil and the European Patent Organisation are now adequate: do not ask for additional Article 46 safeguards for them.
- Do not consider adequate a country with an arrangement that is only in progress (e.g. Kenya).
- The list changes with new decisions and reviews: for a real transfer check the date of the card and, if the recheck date has passed, the source.
