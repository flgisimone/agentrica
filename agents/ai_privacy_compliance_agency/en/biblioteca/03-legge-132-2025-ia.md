# Italian law on artificial intelligence (Law 132/2025)

Source: normattiva · Version: Law no. 132 of 23 September 2025 · Verified on: 2026-11-19 · Recheck by: 2027-05-19 · Roles: AIPCA-01, AIPCA-02, AIPCA-03, AIPCA-08, AIPCA-10

## Notion
Italian Law no. 132 of 23 September 2025, «Provisions and delegations to the Government on artificial intelligence» (in Italian «Disposizioni e deleghe al Governo in materia di intelligenza artificiale»), has been in force since 10 October 2025 and must be interpreted in line with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Among the provisions that affect data processing:

- art. 4, paragraph 4: under the age of fourteen, access to AI technologies and the related data processing require the consent of whoever holds parental responsibility; from fourteen the minor can consent to the processing, if the information is easily accessible and understandable;
- art. 11: the employer, or the client, must inform the worker about the use of AI, according to the rules of art. 1-bis of Legislative Decree 152/1997;
- art. 13: the professional tells the client which AI systems they use, in clear, simple and complete language.

## How it applies
- For an AI service also aimed at minors in Italy, check how consent is collected under fourteen and how the information for minors is written.
- If an AI system is used with workers or contractors, ask where the notice required by art. 11 is.
- The law also contains delegations to the Government: for detailed obligations check the implementing decrees on the source, without assuming them.
