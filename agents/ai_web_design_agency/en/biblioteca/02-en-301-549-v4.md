# ETSI EN 301 549 V4.1.1

Source: etsi-en-301-549 · Version: V4.1.1 · Verified on: 2026-11-21 · Recheck by: 2027-02-21 · Roles: AIWDA-01, AIWDA-08, AIWDA-09

## Notion
The European standard EN 301 549 on accessibility requirements for ICT products and services has a new version, V4.1.1, published by ETSI in September 2026; for the web it refers to WCAG 2.2 AA. As of 21 November 2026 the version cited in the Official Journal of the European Union as a harmonised standard is V3.2.1 (2021-03), with Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1339 for Directive (EU) 2016/2102 on the websites and apps of public sector bodies. There is no citation of V4.1.1, nor a citation of EN 301 549 for Directive (EU) 2019/882, the European Accessibility Act.

## How it applies
- When a project or a contract cites EN 301 549, ask which version: V3.2.1 and V4.1.1 must not be taken as equivalent.
- For the presumption of conformity the reference remains V3.2.1; V4.1.1 can be used as a higher target, saying so.
- Before treating V4.1.1 as a harmonised standard, check on EUR-Lex whether and since when it is cited: the citation is expected, but without an official date.
- The content of the requirements must be read in the text of the standard, not inferred from this card.
