# Accessibility of products and services: Legislative Decree 82/2022

Source: normattiva · Version: Legislative Decree no. 82 of 27 May 2022 · Verified on: 2026-11-21 · Recheck by: 2027-05-21 · Roles: AIWDA-01, AIWDA-03, AIWDA-08, AIWDA-09, AIWDA-10

## Notion
Italian Legislative Decree no. 82 of 27 May 2022 transposes Directive (EU) 2019/882, the European Accessibility Act. From 28 June 2025 the accessibility requirements apply to the products and services listed in the decree placed on the market. Some website and app content is excluded, including pre-recorded time-based media and office file formats published before 28 June 2025 and archive content not updated after that date.

Microenterprises providing services are excluded from the decree: fewer than 10 persons employed and an annual turnover or annual balance sheet total not exceeding 2 million euro (art. 2, paragraph 1, letter bb). Microenterprises dealing with products remain subject to the requirements, but do not have to document the assessment of disproportionate burden or inform the market surveillance authority. Transitional periods: service contracts concluded before 28 June 2025 remain unchanged until they expire, no later than 28 June 2030; until 28 June 2030 service providers may use the products they lawfully used before 28 June 2025; self-service terminals already in use remain until the end of their useful life, at most 20 years after they were put into service. Surveillance: AgID for services, the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy for products; penalties from 5,000 to 40,000 euro for breaching the requirements.

## How it applies
- For a website or app of a private operator offering one of the services in the decree (e.g. e-commerce), accessibility is an applicable obligation, not just good practice.
- Content published or updated after 28 June 2025 does not fall under the exclusions.
- Before saying that a company is exempt as a microenterprise, check both conditions (persons employed and financial threshold) and that it offers only services.
- The precise articles on transitional periods and exemptions must be read in the text on Normattiva: the card summarises them from AgID's frequently asked questions.
